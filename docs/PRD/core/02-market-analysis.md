## Market Segments & Strategy

### Segment 1: Tax Consultants (Primary Target)

**Why Target First**:
- Highest ARPU: Rp 2.5M/month
- Multiplier effect: 1 consultant = 35 clients
- Validates product across all user types
- B2B2C channel (consultant brings clients)

**Market Size**: 10,000 consultants in Indonesia

**Pain Points**:
- 70% of time spent on data collection
- Can't scale beyond 35 clients
- Email chaos (version control issues)
- Manual DJP submission (35x login/logout)

**Solution Value**:
- 87% time savings (80 → 10 hours/month)
- Scale to 50+ clients (+50% revenue)
- Real-time collaboration portal
- Bulk submission (8 minutes vs 8 hours)

---

### Segment 2: UMKM (Largest TAM)

**Market Size**: 64M businesses (Rp 15T TAM)

**Pain Points**:
- Forgets monthly 15th deadline → Rp 100K penalties
- Doesn't know about Year 3→4 transition
- Rp 8M/year consultant fees (unaffordable)

**Solution Value**:
- Save Rp 5.6M/year (vs Rp 8M consultant)
- Zero missed deadlines (reminders)
- Prepared for Year 4 tax increase

**Acquisition Channels**:
- TikTok/Instagram ads
- Consultant referrals
- E-commerce platform partnerships (Shopee, TikTok Shop)

---

### Segment 3: Corporate (PT) (Highest Value)

**Market Size**: 1.5M companies (Rp 18T TAM)

**Pain Points**:
- 600 hours/year manual work
- Rp 51M/year consultant fees
- Duplicate data entry (Accurate → Excel → DJP)

**Solution Value**:
- Save Rp 21M/year + 516 hours
- Accurate integration (eliminate duplication)
- 95% PPN auto-reconciliation

**Acquisition Channels**:
- LinkedIn B2B sales
- Accounting software partnerships (Accurate, Zahir)
- Consultant referrals

---

### Segment 4: Individual (Mass Market)

**Market Size**: 40M taxpayers (Rp 2T TAM)

**Pain Points**:

**Solution Value**:

## Market Entry Barriers

### High Barriers
- **DJP API access**: Requires official certification
  - **Mitigation**: Partner with Jakarta Tax Consulting (PJAP certified)
- **User trust**: Tax is sensitive
  - **Mitigation**: Transparent legal structure (Jakarta Tax Consulting handles filing)

### Medium Barriers
- **Network effects**: Consultants have existing client relationships
  - **Mitigation**: Offer superior tools → consultants switch voluntarily
- **Integration complexity**: Accurate, e-Faktur, banks
  - **Mitigation**: Phased rollout (P0 → P1 → P2)

### Low Barriers
- **Technology**: SaaS infrastructure is commoditized
  - **Advantage**: NestJS + React + AWS = fast development, scalable infrastructure
