## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Tax Consultants First (Months 1-6)

**Why Consultants First?**
1. **Multiplier Effect**: 1 consultant = 35 clients
2. **Validation**: Tests product across all user types
3. **Highest ARPU**: Rp 2.5M/month
4. **B2B2C Channel**: Consultants bring clients

**Channels**:
- Tax consultant associations (IKPI)
- Industry seminars: "Double Clients with AI, Save 50% Time"
- First 10 consultants: Free for 6 months

**Content**:
- "Manage 50 clients, not 35"
- "87% time savings case study"
- "Bulk submit 35 clients in 8 minutes"

**Goal**: 200 consultants signed → 7,000 end-clients influenced

---

### Phase 2: UMKM Attack (Months 6-12)

**Channels**:
- **TikTok Ads**: "File taxes in 5 minutes!"
- **Instagram**: Influencer partnerships (online seller community)
- **Shopee/TikTok Shop**: Platform partnerships
- **Consultant Referrals**: Existing consultant clients

**Content**:
- "Avoid Rp 1.2M tax penalties"
- "Year 3→4 transition warning" (viral content)
- "Save Rp 5.6M/year vs consultant"
- Video tutorials: Receipt scanning, SPT filing

**Promotion**: First 3 months free (then Rp 150K/month)

**Goal**: 10,000 UMKM businesses

---

### Phase 3: Corporate Expansion (Months 12-24)

**Channels**:
- **LinkedIn**: B2B sales (target HRD/CFO)
- **Webinars**: "90% Payroll Time Savings"
- **Accurate/Zahir Partnerships**: Integration co-marketing
- **Consultant Referrals**: Consultants introduce to corporate clients

**Message**:
- "Accurate integration → no duplicate entry"
- "Save Rp 21M/year"
- "516 hours saved annually"

**Sales Cycle**: 30-60 days (demo → trial → contract)

**Goal**: 2,000 corporate clients

---

### Phase 4: Individual Mass Market (Months 18-36)

**Channels**:
- **Google Ads**: March tax season (high intent)
- **SEO**: "SPT filing Indonesia", "Form 1721-A1"
- **TV Ads**: March peak season
- **Influencers**: Personal finance YouTubers

**Message**:
- "File SPT in 3 minutes, not 2 hours"
- "Free EFIN password recovery"
- "Guaranteed tax refund"

**Goal**: 50,000 individual taxpayers

## Payment Gateway Strategy

### Midtrans Virtual Account (Primary)
**Cost**: Rp 4,000 flat per transaction
**Best For**: UMKM, Corporate (Rp 150K-1M subscriptions)
**Annual PG Cost**: ~2.3% of revenue (very efficient)

### Xendit (Enterprise)
**Cost**: T+0 settlement, bank transfer (Rp 0 fee)
**Best For**: Corporate, Consultant (Rp 1M+ subscriptions)

### QRIS (Micro-payments)
**Cost**: 0.7%
**Best For**: Top-ups, one-time purchases

## Unit Economics

### UMKM Segment (Primary Growth Driver)

**ARPU**: Rp 2.4M/year
**CAC**: Rp 600K (consultant referral) to Rp 1.2M (paid ads)
**LTV**: Rp 12M (assuming 5-year retention)
**LTV:CAC**: 10:1 (consultant referral), 5:1 (paid ads)
**Payback Period**: 3-6 months

**Gross Margin**: 85% (SaaS model)

---

### Consultant Segment (Highest ROI)

**ARPU**: Rp 30M/year
**CAC**: Rp 3M (direct sales, seminars)
**LTV**: Rp 150M (assuming 5-year retention)
**LTV:CAC**: 50:1
**Payback Period**: 1 month

**Multiplier Effect**: Each consultant brings 35 clients → Rp 84M additional revenue

**Gross Margin**: 90% (minimal support cost)
