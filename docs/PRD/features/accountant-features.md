## P0 Features (MVP)

1. **Bank Account Integration**: Auto-import monthly revenue from bank settlements
2. **PPh Final 0.5% Auto-Calc**: Revenue x 0.5% automatic calculation
3. **Monthly Reminders**: D-5 and D-1 notifications before 15th deadline
4. **e-Billing Generation**: One-click ID Billing for tax payment
5. **SPT Masa Filing**: One-click monthly SPT submission
6. **Year 3→4 Transition Alert**: Proactive warning about tax rate increase
7. **Penalty Avoidance**: Track compliance to avoid Rp 100K/month penalties

## P1 Features (Phase 2)

8. **Receipt OCR**: Scan receipts for expense tracking (Year 4 preparation)
9. **E-commerce Integration**: Direct TikTok Shop/Shopee revenue sync
10. **SPT Tahunan Auto-Gen**: Annual tax return automation
11. **Tax Optimization**: Tips to reduce tax in Year 4+
