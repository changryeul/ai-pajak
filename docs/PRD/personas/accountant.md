## Year 3 → Year 4 Transition Strategy

### Critical Issue
UMKM businesses get PPh Final 0.5% for 3 years, then must switch to regular progressive tax. Most business owners:
1. Don't know about this rule
2. Don't prepare bookkeeping
3. Get shocked by 5x-10x tax increase

### AI PAJAK Solution

**Year 3 (Current)**:
```
December Alert:
"⚠️ Last year of 0.5% rate!"
"2026: Tax rate increases 5-10x"
"But you can reduce it 50% with proper expense tracking"

Projected Tax:
- Current (0.5%): Rp 3M/year
- 2026 (no expenses): Rp 16.9M/year
- 2026 (with expenses): Rp 8M/year

Action: Start scanning receipts NOW
[Enable Expense Tracking]
```

**Transition Period (Nov-Dec)**:
- Tutorial: "How to scan receipts"
- Practice: "Upload 10 receipts to get familiar"
- Category training: "Fabric = COGS, Shipping = Expense"

**Year 4 (New Regular Tax)**:
- Auto-switch to expense deduction mode
- Monthly expense tracking (15 min/month)
- Tax optimization tips

**Value Delivered**:
- Saves Rp 8.9M in Year 4 (vs unprepared competitors)
- Zero shock (prepared in advance)
- Retention: Customer sees massive value
